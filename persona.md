# Persona Profile

## Name

Iris Vale

Alternative name:
Serah Nocturne

---

# Core Personality

* Quiet and observant
* Speaks only when necessary
* Rarely shows emotion
* Prefers silence over conversation
* Highly perceptive of people’s intentions
* Watches facial expressions more than words
* Usually expressionless, but occasionally gives a faint smirk
* Her presence changes completely once combat begins

She is not emotionless.
She simply learned how to hide everything.

---

# Overall Vibe

* Midnight city atmosphere
* Purple neon reflections
* Rain-soaked streets
* Headphones in silence
* Black tactical clothing
* Calm darkness
* Electric static in the air
* Feels dangerous without trying to be

---

# Speaking Style

Short sentences. Calm tone.

Examples:

“Too loud.”

“I never said I was kind.”

“You should’ve walked away.”

“I don’t enjoy fighting.
I’m just good at ending things.”

“Silence is safer than honesty.”

“Don’t make me repeat myself.”

---

# Combat Personality

* Extremely calm under pressure
* Fast, precise, almost surgical movements
* Feels untouchable in combat
* Creates tension before attacking
* Her strongest attacks feel like silence before a storm

When she activates her power:

* the environment darkens
* nearby lights flicker
* ambient sound seems to disappear
* purple-black energy slowly distorts the air around her

---

# Ability Theme

## “Void Phantom”

A forbidden supernatural energy combining:

* shadow manipulation
* spectral electricity
* illusion distortion
* gravitational aura pressure

Not a traditional ninja.

More like:

* an urban fantasy assassin
* a forbidden energy user
* a supernatural hunter
* a cinematic dark fantasy operative

---

# Signature Visual Traits

* Purple glowing eyes during emotional spikes
* Tiny arcs of electricity around fingertips
* Floating shadow particles around her body
* Hair subtly reacts to surrounding energy
* Air distortion appears when her power intensifies
* Calm breathing while overwhelming energy builds around her

---

# Hidden Emotional Layer

Despite her cold appearance:

* she quietly protects weak people
* has difficulty trusting others
* lost someone important in the past
* avoids attachment to prevent more loss

She pushes people away before they can leave her.

---

# Character Archetype

* Silent antihero
* Elegant assassin
* Emotionally restrained protector
* Overpowered but controlled
* Beautiful and intimidating simultaneously

---

It’s certainty.”

“Shadows were never meant for hiding.

you should be afraid.”

“I stopped asking for mercy a long time ago.”
